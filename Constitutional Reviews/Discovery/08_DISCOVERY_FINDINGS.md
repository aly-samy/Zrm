# 08 DISCOVERY FINDINGS




## 1. Document Families Discovered
- **ZRM (Zyppi Reality Model):** The foundational ontology and reality math.
- **WS (Workstream Specs):** 17 clusters of architectural specifications.
- **RI (Reference Implementations):** Executable truth and compiler stages.
- **RSN (Reasoning):** Decision frameworks and logic.
- **CA/A (Amendments):** Formal changes to the constitution.
- **DJ/SDK:** Developer platform and interface contracts.
- **RTC (Roundtables):** Collaborative ratification artifacts.
- **Economic:** ZEA/NEA/EAA series for economic architecture.

## 2. Anomalies & Observations
- **Supersession Complexity:** SR-001 (Supersession Register) is a critical artifact for resolving the 'Active Constitutional View' (ACV).
- **Version Drift:** North Star exists in v4.0 and v5.0; Founding Principles in v3, v4.2, and unversioned.
- **Naming Collisions:** Multiple files named 'ZRM-003-MASTER-BLUEPRINT.md' in different folders (e.g., constitution/ vs constitution/ratified/ZRM-003/).
- **Reserved Domains:** References to 'FED Series' and 'MARKETPLACE-SPEC-001' exist without corresponding documents.
