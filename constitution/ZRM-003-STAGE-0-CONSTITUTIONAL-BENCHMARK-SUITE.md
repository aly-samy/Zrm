**Purpose**: This becomes the gate that every future revision of ZRM-003 must pass before mathematics can be changed.
 
Its job is **not** to define mathematics.
 
Its job is to define **how mathematics will be validated.**

# 8. Constitutional Success Criteria
 
Stage 0 succeeds only when all of the following are true.
 
### SC-1
 
Every benchmark maps entirely to the five Constitutional Primitives.
 
### SC-2
 
Every benchmark maps entirely to the Structural Constitutional Constructs.
 
### SC-3
 
No benchmark requires an additional Primitive.
 
### SC-4
 
No benchmark requires an additional Structural Construct.
 
### SC-5
 
All mathematical requirements are identified.
 
### SC-6
 
No implementation concepts are introduced.
 
### SC-7
 
No benchmark contradicts another benchmark.
 
### SC-8
 
Collectively, the benchmark suite exercises every mathematical domain required by ZRM-003.
  
# 9. Constitutional Failure Conditions
 
Stage 0 immediately fails if any of the following occur.
 
## FC-1
 
A benchmark cannot be represented using existing Constitutional Primitives.
 
## FC-2
 
A benchmark requires a new Structural Construct.
 
## FC-3
 
Two benchmarks require contradictory mathematical behavior.
 
## FC-4
 
A benchmark requires implementation-specific assumptions.
 
## FC-5
 
A benchmark exposes missing constitutional mathematics.

# 11. Constitutional Exit Criteria
 
Stage 0 is complete only when:
 
 
- Every witness has been evaluated.
 
- Every evaluation has been approved by the Council.
 
- The Constitutional Coverage Matrix is fully satisfied.
 
- No constitutional gaps remain.
 
- No additional primitives or structural constructs are required.
 
- The Council formally certifies the benchmark suite as complete.
 

 
Only then may Stage 1 (Constitutional Mathematical Vocabulary) begin.
  
# 12. Constitutional Lock
 
Upon ratification:
 
 
- The Constitutional Benchmark Suite becomes the permanent validation framework for all Reality Mathematics.
 
- Every future amendment to ZRM-003 SHALL be validated against this benchmark suite before ratification.
 
- No mathematical amendment may bypass Stage 0.
 
- The Constitutional Coverage Matrix becomes a permanent governance artifact.
 

  
## One addition I would make beyond the Council's recommendations
 
I would add an eleventh benchmark:
 
**Natural Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)**
 
Reason: ZRM is called the **Reality Model**, not the Enterprise Model. If your constitutional mathematics can represent molecules, ecosystems, astronomical bodies, and biological evolution using the same primitives, it provides even stronger evidence that the ontology is genuinely universal rather than optimized for information systems. Even if this benchmark is evaluated at a conceptual level, it broadens the validation beyond software and enterprise domains without introducing new primitives.
