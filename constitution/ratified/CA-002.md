# Purpose
This amendment resolves the constitutional ambiguity surrounding the concept of Campaign within the Zyppi Reality Model (ZRM).

Historically, Campaign appeared in multiple constitutional roles:
- As a Constitutional Primitive in WS-02
- As an Identity-bearing business object
- As a Contextual execution envelope

Subsequent constitutional development demonstrated that these represent distinct realities rather than a single constitutional object.

This amendment formally retires Campaign as a Constitutional Primitive and establishes the canonical dual-model of Campaign Identity and Campaign Context.

# Section 1 — Constitutional Finding
The Council determined that Campaign performs two fundamentally different functions within ZRM:

## Function A — Identity
A Campaign may possess:
- A name
- A lifecycle
- An owner
- A budget
- A business purpose

**Governance responsibility**
These characteristics make Campaign a valid Identity-bearing object.

## Function B — Context
A Campaign may simultaneously define:
- Audience scope
- Channel scope
- Geographic scope
- Time scope
- Experimental conditions
- Routing conditions

These characteristics make Campaign a Contextual execution envelope.

These functions are distinct and SHALL NOT be represented by a single constitutional object.

# Section 2 — Retirement of Campaign as Constitutional Primitive
Any prior constitutional language describing Campaign as a standalone Constitutional Primitive is hereby superseded.

**Campaign SHALL NOT exist as:**
- A Primitive
- A Root Ontological Category
- A Registry Cluster
- A First-Class Constitutional Layer

Campaign is henceforth represented through existing constitutional structures.

# Section 3 — Campaign Identity
Campaign Identity SHALL be governed by CL-04 Identity.

Campaign Identity represents the business-recognizable campaign artifact.

**Examples include:**
- Ramadan 2027 Promotion
- Summer Launch Initiative
- Customer Retention Campaign
- Product Recall Communication Program

Campaign Identity may possess:
- Identity Identifier
- Name
- Description
- Ownership
- Lifecycle State
- Organizational Accountability
- Business Metadata

Campaign Identity answers:
> "What campaign are we talking about?"

# Section 4 — Campaign Context
Campaign Context SHALL be governed by CL-16 Intelligence.

Campaign Context represents the execution conditions surrounding a campaign.

Campaign Context may include:
- Audience Conditions
- Geographic Conditions
- Temporal Conditions
- Language Conditions
- Device Conditions
- Channel Conditions
- Experimental Conditions
- Segmentation Conditions

Campaign Context answers:
> "Under what conditions does this campaign operate?"

Campaign Context SHALL NOT be treated as an Identity.

Campaign Context SHALL NOT possess independent business ownership.

Campaign Context SHALL function as an execution modifier only.

# Section 5 — Context/Identity Separation Rule
Campaign Identity and Campaign Context SHALL remain constitutionally independent.

A Campaign Identity may reference one or more Campaign Contexts.

A Campaign Context may be reused by multiple Campaign Identities where appropriate.

Neither object inherits ownership from the other.

Neither object supersedes the other.

Neither object serves as the parent of the other.

The relationship between them is associative rather than hierarchical.

# Section 6 — Canonical Mapping Rule
The following constitutional mapping is hereby established:

Concept | Governing Cluster
---|---
Campaign Identity | CL-04 Identity
Campaign Context | CL-16 Intelligence
Campaign Performance Events | CL-11 Event
Campaign Transactions | CL-09 Transaction
Campaign Outcomes | CL-10 Outcome
Campaign Compliance Constraints | CL-14 Compliance

No Campaign-related object may be assigned to an alternative cluster unless explicitly defined by a future constitutional amendment.

# Section 7 — Migration and Interpretation Guidance
All existing and future constitutional documents SHALL interpret references to Campaign according to the following rule:

---

**When the reference concerns:**
- Ownership
- Lifecycle
- Governance
- Accountability
- Business Recognition

Interpret Campaign as Campaign Identity (CL-04).

---

**When the reference concerns:**
- Routing
- Segmentation
- Audience Selection
- Execution Conditions
- Evaluation Conditions
- Contextual Scope

Interpret Campaign as Campaign Context (CL-16).

---

Where ambiguity exists, the author SHALL explicitly specify which form is intended.

# Section 8 — Interaction with Unified Context Amendment
This amendment is designed to operate alongside CA-003 Unified Context Model.

Campaign Context SHALL be considered one possible contextual envelope within the broader Context framework.

Campaign Context does not establish a new context type.

Campaign Context is an application of Context principles rather than a separate constitutional concept.

# Section 9 — Effective Result
**Upon ratification:**
- Campaign is no longer a Constitutional Primitive.
- Campaign Identity is formally assigned to CL-04.
- Campaign Context is formally assigned to CL-16.

Constitutional ambiguity regarding Campaign placement is resolved.

All future registry population SHALL follow this dual-model.
