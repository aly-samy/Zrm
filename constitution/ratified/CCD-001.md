# CCD-001 — WS-03A.0 / WS-04A Co-Dependency Clarification
---|---
Status: | Ratified Clarification

## Purpose: 
Resolve CIA-02 Finding CR-008 concerning apparent forward dependency.

## Finding
- WS-03A.0 references WS-04A as a prerequisite.
- WS-04A implicitly depends on cluster ownership definitions established by WS-03A.0.

This creates an apparent forward-reference anomaly.

## Resolution
The relationship is not a prerequisite dependency.

The relationship is a Constitutional Co-Dependency.

### WS-03A.0 provides:
- Cluster ownership boundaries
- Registry ownership rules
### WS-04A provides:
- Relationship classification
- Relationship governance

Neither document can fully express constitutional reality without the other.

## Constitutional Rule
**For dependency mapping purposes:**
`WS-03A.0` ↔ `WS-04A`

shall be recorded as:
- CO-DEPENDENT

not

- PREREQUISITE

This finding is closed.
