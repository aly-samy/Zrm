# Constitutional Amendment CA-004
# Authority Revocation and Delegation Lifecycle Amendment
---|---
Status | Ratified Constitutional Amendment
Priority | P0 – Governance Completion

# Purpose
This amendment formally defines the lifecycle states of Authority Anchors and establishes constitutional rules governing suspension, revocation, and delegation termination.

The amendment resolves CIA-02 Finding CR-005 and closes a governance gap identified in WS-03D Authority Governance.

This amendment introduces no new clusters, registries, or constitutional primitives.

# Section 1 — Constitutional Principle
Authority within ZRM is delegated authority.

Authority exists only through a valid Authority Anchor and remains valid only while the Authority Anchor remains active.

Authority may be suspended or revoked without altering historical reality.

Historical transactions remain valid if committed under a previously valid authority state.

# Section 2 — Authority Anchor Lifecycle
Every Authority Anchor SHALL exist in exactly one lifecycle state.

## AA-01 Active
An Active Authority Anchor:
- MAY issue authority
- MAY validate Role Assignments
- MAY authorize Transactions
- MAY authorize Intent Contracts
- MAY activate Relationships requiring authority validation

An Active Authority Anchor is considered constitutionally valid.

## AA-02 Suspended
A Suspended Authority Anchor:
- SHALL NOT authorize new Transactions
- SHALL NOT activate new Intent Contracts
- SHALL NOT validate new Role Assignments
- SHALL retain historical authority records

Suspension is temporary and reversible.

A Suspended Authority Anchor MAY later return to Active status.

## AA-03 Revoked
A Revoked Authority Anchor:
- SHALL permanently lose authority validity
- SHALL NOT authorize any future activity
- SHALL NOT return to Active status
- SHALL remain preserved for historical auditability

Revocation is permanent.

A Revoked Authority Anchor MAY only be superseded by creation of a new Authority Anchor.

Historical records SHALL NOT be modified.

# Section 3 — Delegation Cascade Rules
Authority delegation forms a dependency chain.
````
Authority Anchor → Role Assignment → Intent Contract → Transaction Authorization
````
When an Authority Anchor changes state, dependent authority objects SHALL be evaluated.

## DC-01 Suspension Cascade
When an Authority Anchor enters Suspended state:

All dependent Role Assignments SHALL automatically enter Suspended state.

**Suspended Role Assignments:**
- SHALL NOT authorize new actions
- SHALL retain historical records
- MAY return to Active if the Authority Anchor returns to Active

## DC-02 Revocation Cascade
When an Authority Anchor enters Revoked state:

All dependent Role Assignments SHALL automatically enter Revoked state.

**Revoked Role Assignments:**
- SHALL NOT authorize future actions
- SHALL NOT return to Active
- SHALL remain historically preserved
- Revocation SHALL propagate through all delegation chains.

# Section 4 — Transaction Handling
This amendment distinguishes between:
- committed reality
- pending execution

## TX-01 Committed Transactions
Transactions committed before suspension or revocation SHALL remain valid.
No retroactive invalidation SHALL occur.
No committed Transaction SHALL be modified.

## TX-02 Pending Transactions
Transactions not yet committed SHALL undergo authority validation.

If the required Authority Anchor is:
- `Active` → execution MAY continue 
- `Suspended` → execution SHALL pause or fail according to Contract rules
- `Revoked` → execution SHALL fail
Failure SHALL be recorded as an Event.

# Section 5 — Intent Contract Effects
Intent Contracts derive execution authority from Authority Anchors.

When an Authority Anchor becomes Suspended:
- existing Contracts MAY complete if execution has already begun
- no new execution instances SHALL be created

When an Authority Anchor becomes Revoked:
- no new execution instances SHALL be created
- authority validation SHALL fail

Intent Contracts themselves remain immutable historical objects.

# Section 6 — Event Recording
Every lifecycle transition SHALL generate an Event.

**Required Event Types:**
- AUTHORITY_ACTIVATED
- AUTHORITY_SUSPENDED
- AUTHORITY_RESTORED
- AUTHORITY_REVOKED

These Events SHALL belong to CL-11 Event.
Authority lifecycle Events SHALL reference:
- Authority Anchor
- effective timestamp
- triggering authority source
- justification reference

# Section 7 — Supersession Rules
Authority Revocation SHALL NOT mutate existing Authority Anchors.

Lifecycle changes SHALL be represented through supersession-compatible records.

Historical states SHALL remain queryable.

Authority history MUST remain reconstructable through bitemporal analysis.

# Section 8 — Constitutional Effects
**This amendment introduces:**
- Authority Anchor lifecycle states
- Delegation cascade rules
- Pending Transaction handling
- Authority Event requirements

This amendment does NOT introduce:
- new clusters
- new constitutional primitives
- new registry classes

# Ratification Outcome
CIA-02 Finding CR-005 is resolved.

Authority governance is now complete across:
- Grant
- Validation
- Delegation
- Suspension
- Revocation
- Auditability

**The Authority Model is constitutionally closed.**
