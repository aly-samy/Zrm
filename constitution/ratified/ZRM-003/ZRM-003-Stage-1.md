Stage 1 establishes the constitutional language of mathematics.

# Constitutional Vocabulary Rules

## CR-1 — One Meaning

Every constitutional term SHALL possess exactly one constitutional meaning.

Alternative meanings are prohibited.

---

## CR-2 — No Synonyms

Each constitutional concept SHALL have exactly one canonical term.

Different terms SHALL NOT represent the same constitutional concept.

---

## CR-3 — No Overloaded Terms

A constitutional term SHALL never possess different meanings in different constitutional documents.

Its meaning SHALL remain globally constant.

---

## CR-4 — Technology Independence

Constitutional vocabulary SHALL remain independent of:

- Databases
- Programming languages
- Frameworks
- Cloud platforms
- Storage engines
- Networking protocols
- Vendor technologies

---

## CR-5 — Implementation Independence

Constitutional vocabulary SHALL describe mathematical concepts only.

Implementation behavior SHALL never appear within constitutional definitions.

---

## CR-6 — Constitutional Ancestry

Every constitutional term SHALL trace its authority through the constitutional hierarchy.

```
Stage 1
      ↓
ZRM-002
      ↓
ZRM-001
```

No constitutional term may exist without constitutional ancestry.

---

## CR-7 — No Circular Definitions

A constitutional definition SHALL never depend directly or indirectly upon itself.

Circular definitions are constitutionally invalid.

---

## CR-8 — Vocabulary Freeze

Once ratified, a constitutional definition SHALL remain immutable.

Definitions may only change through the Constitutional Amendment Procedure defined by the ZRM Constitutional Governance framework.

---

## CR-9 — Boundary Ownership

Every constitutional term SHALL have exactly one constitutional owner.

For the mathematical vocabulary defined by ZRM-003, the **ZRM-003 Vocabulary Registry** SHALL be the exclusive constitutional owner of all foundational mathematical terms.

Downstream constitutional documents MAY reference these terms but SHALL NOT:

- redefine them;
- extend their constitutional meaning;
- overload their meaning;
- duplicate their constitutional definition.

New foundational mathematical terms SHALL be introduced only through the Vocabulary Registry using the Constitutional Amendment Procedure.
