# 1. Purpose
 
This document establishes the constitutional process for extracting candidate invariants from ratified mathematical theorems.
 
Invariant Extraction is a **discovery process**, not a creative process.
 
Its purpose is to identify preservation laws that already exist within ratified mathematics and prepare them for constitutional classification.
 
No new mathematical truth may be introduced during extraction.

# 4. Constitutional Principles
 
## Principle 1 — Discovery, Not Creation
 
Extraction SHALL discover preservation laws already embedded within ratified mathematics.
 
Extraction SHALL NOT invent mathematics.
 
Extraction SHALL NOT strengthen mathematics.
 
Extraction SHALL NOT weaken mathematics.
  
## Principle 2 — Complete Traceability
 
Every extracted candidate SHALL trace completely to its mathematical origin.
 
Every candidate SHALL identify:
 
 
- originating theorem(s)
 
- originating mathematical family
 
- proof reference
 
- proof step(s)
 
- extraction rationale
 

 
Candidates without complete provenance SHALL be rejected.
  
## Principle 3 — Mathematical Fidelity
 
Extraction SHALL preserve the exact mathematical meaning of the originating theorem.
 
Extraction SHALL NOT:
 
 
- reinterpret mathematics
 
- broaden mathematical scope
 
- narrow mathematical scope
 
- alter mathematical intent
 

 
If broader applicability is desired, the originating theorem SHALL first be revised within Stage 4.
 
Only mathematically proven **isomorphic mappings** may preserve scope without requiring theorem revision.
  
## Principle 4 — Family Neutrality
 
The extraction methodology SHALL be identical for every mathematical family.
 
No family receives special treatment.
 
Current families include:
 
 
- Identity Mathematics
 
- Event Mathematics
 
- Time Mathematics
 
- State Mathematics
 

 
Future mathematical families SHALL follow this same process.
  
## Principle 5 — Repeatability
 
Independent constitutional reviewers examining identical theorem registries SHALL discover substantially equivalent candidate invariants.
 
Extraction SHALL therefore be:
 
 
- deterministic
 
- reproducible
 
- reviewable
 
- auditable

# 8. Preservation Property Identification Methodology
 
Extraction is performed by examining mathematical proofs for preservation laws.
 
Reviewers SHALL identify:
 
 
- assumptions preserved throughout the proof
 
- mathematical quantities proven invariant
 
- preserved relationships
 
- preserved identities
 
- preserved ordering
 
- preserved uniqueness
 
- preserved causality
 
- preserved consistency
 

 
Extraction identifies preservation.
 
It does not derive new mathematics.
  
## 8.1 Structural Extraction Patterns
 
Primary extraction SHALL rely upon mathematical structure rather than linguistic phrasing.
 
Reviewers SHALL identify patterns such as:
 
### Conservation
 
A mathematical property remains unchanged.
 `f(x) = f(T(x)) `  
### Identity Preservation
 
Identity survives every valid transformation.
  
### Monotonicity
 
A mathematical property evolves in only one constitutional direction.
  
### Immutability
 
A constitutional object cannot change after establishment.
  
### Unreachability
 
Certain mathematical states are impossible.
  
### Boundary Constraints
 
Mathematical boundaries cannot be crossed.
  
### Dependency Preservation
 
The validity of one theorem depends upon preservation established by another theorem.
  
These structural patterns constitute the primary extraction methodology.
  
## 8.2 Reviewer Guidance
 
Natural language expressions may assist reviewers but SHALL NEVER constitute mathematical evidence.
 
Examples include:
 
 
- always
 
- never
 
- immutable
 
- deterministic
 
- preserved
 
- remains unchanged
 
- equivalent
 
- irreversible
 

 
These are reviewer hints only.
 
They are not constitutional proof.

# 9. Extraction Record

Every extracted candidate SHALL be recorded before entering Stage 5C.

No candidate may proceed without a complete Extraction Record.

The Extraction Record establishes the constitutional provenance of every candidate invariant.

---

## 9.1 Mandatory Record Fields

Every Extraction Record SHALL contain the following information.

| Field | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Candidate ID | Temporary constitutional identifier |
| Candidate Statement | Human-readable preservation law |
| Origin Family | Identity, Event, Time, State, or Cross-Family |
| Origin Theorem(s) | Exact theorem identifiers |
| Proof Reference | Exact proof section(s) |
| Proof Step(s) | Exact proof step(s) revealing the preservation property |
| Mathematical Pattern | Conservation, Immutability, Unreachability, etc. |
| Extraction Rationale | Why this is a preservation law |
| Initial Scope | Family, Cross-Family, or Foundational candidate |
| Reviewer | Constitutional reviewer |
| Extraction Date | Date of extraction |
| Confidence Level | Direct, Composite, or Interpretive |
| Status | Pending Classification |

---

## 9.2 Extraction Record Requirements

Every field SHALL be complete.

Missing provenance SHALL invalidate the candidate.

Unknown origin SHALL invalidate the candidate.

Ambiguous proof references SHALL invalidate the candidate.

Incomplete records SHALL NOT enter Stage 5C.

---

# 10. Provenance Requirements

Every candidate invariant SHALL possess complete mathematical provenance.

Provenance guarantees constitutional traceability.

---

## 10.1 Minimum Provenance

Every candidate SHALL identify:

- originating theorem ID
- originating mathematical family
- proof section
- proof step
- preservation property discovered

---

## 10.2 Composite Provenance

Some preservation laws emerge only from multiple theorems.

In such cases the Extraction Record SHALL identify every contributing theorem.

Example

TH-I004

+

TH-E007

↓

Candidate CI-024

No contributing theorem may be omitted.

---

## 10.3 Exact Proof References

Acceptable references include:

TH-I003 — Proof Step 4

TH-E001 — Section 3.2

TH-T005 — Lemma 2

TH-S008 — Corollary A

General references such as

"Identity proofs"

or

"Time mathematics"

are constitutionally insufficient.

---

## 10.4 Provenance Integrity

Every reviewer SHALL be capable of reconstructing the extraction directly from the recorded provenance.

Extraction SHALL therefore be:

- reproducible
- independently verifiable
- mathematically auditable

---

# 11. Candidate Confidence Levels

Not every extraction possesses identical certainty.

Confidence levels indicate extraction maturity.

They do not indicate constitutional validity.

---

## 11.1 Direct

The preservation law is explicitly demonstrated by a single theorem.

No interpretation is required.

Highest confidence.

---

## 11.2 Composite

The preservation law emerges from several theorem proofs.

Each contributing theorem is individually proven.

The preservation law becomes evident only through their composition.

---

## 11.3 Interpretive

The preservation property appears strongly supported but requires constitutional review before acceptance.

Interpretive candidates SHALL receive additional scrutiny during Stage 5C.

Interpretive candidates SHALL NEVER bypass constitutional review.

---

# 12. Duplicate Detection

Different reviewers may discover equivalent preservation laws.

Equivalent discoveries SHALL be consolidated before classification.

---

## 12.1 Duplicate Candidates

Two candidates are duplicates if they express identical constitutional preservation.

Only one SHALL remain.

---

## 12.2 Equivalent Mathematical Expressions

Different mathematical notation describing the same preservation law SHALL be considered duplicates.

Notation SHALL NOT create multiple invariants.

---

## 12.3 Subsumption Rule

A candidate subsumes another candidate only if:

- it preserves identical constitutional meaning;
- it possesses equal or stronger mathematical proof; and
- it derives from the same or stronger provenance.

If these conditions are not satisfied, both candidates SHALL remain distinct.

---

## 12.4 Generalization Restrictions

Extraction SHALL NOT broaden theorem scope.

If broader applicability is desired:

1. return to Stage 4;
2. revise the originating theorem;
3. ratify the revised theorem;
4. repeat extraction.

Generalization SHALL NEVER occur during Stage 5.

---

## 12.5 Merge Rules

Merged candidates SHALL preserve:

- complete provenance;
- every originating theorem;
- every proof reference;
- every proof step.

No provenance may be discarded during consolidation.

---

# 13. Cross-Family Extraction

Some preservation laws emerge only through interaction between mathematical families.

These candidates SHALL be explicitly identified.

---

## 13.1 Cross-Family Analysis

Reviewers SHALL examine every interaction between:

- Identity ↔ Event
- Identity ↔ Time
- Identity ↔ State
- Event ↔ Time
- Event ↔ State
- Time ↔ State

Cross-family preservation often reveals higher-order constitutional guarantees.

---

## 13.2 Boundary Interaction Matrix

Every cross-family review SHALL identify:

- preserved mathematical boundaries;
- preserved dependencies;
- preserved ordering;
- preserved causality;
- preserved identity relationships;
- impossible cross-family states.

Boundary analysis SHALL accompany every Cross-Family candidate.

---

## 13.3 Foundational Candidate Detection

If a preservation law appears across every mathematical family, it SHALL be marked as a **Foundational Candidate**.

Foundational Candidates are not automatically accepted.

They proceed through the same constitutional workflow as every other candidate.

Potential designation as a Foundational Invariant occurs only after successful classification and ratification.

# 14. Quality Gates

Every extracted candidate SHALL satisfy every Quality Gate before entering Stage 5C.

Quality Gates ensure that only constitutionally valid candidates proceed.

Failure of any Quality Gate SHALL reject the candidate for correction.

---

## QG-1 Mathematical Origin

The candidate SHALL derive entirely from ratified mathematics.

Acceptable sources include:

- ratified theorems
- ratified lemmas
- ratified corollaries
- proven cross-theorem relationships

No other sources are permitted.

---

## QG-2 Complete Provenance

Every candidate SHALL possess complete constitutional provenance.

Required provenance includes:

- theorem identifiers
- proof references
- proof steps
- mathematical family
- extraction rationale

Incomplete provenance SHALL invalidate the candidate.

---

## QG-3 Preservation Property

The extracted statement SHALL express a preservation law.

Examples include:

- conservation
- continuity
- immutability
- monotonicity
- ordering
- determinism
- impossibility
- boundedness

If the statement merely describes a mathematical consequence, it belongs to Stage 4, not Stage 5.

---

## QG-4 Implementation Independence

The candidate SHALL contain no implementation assumptions.

The candidate SHALL NOT reference:

- databases
- APIs
- programming languages
- execution engines
- storage models
- serialization
- infrastructure
- networking
- hardware

Only constitutional mathematics may remain.

---

## QG-5 Mathematical Consistency

The candidate SHALL NOT contradict:

- axioms
- definitions
- theorems
- existing invariants

Any contradiction SHALL initiate the Constitutional Rejection Cascade.

---

## QG-6 Duplicate Resolution

Equivalent candidates SHALL be consolidated before classification.

Subsumption SHALL follow Section 12.

Duplicate constitutional statements SHALL NOT enter the registry.

---

## QG-7 Classification Readiness

The candidate SHALL contain sufficient information for constitutional classification.

Candidates with unresolved ambiguity SHALL return for further extraction.

---
