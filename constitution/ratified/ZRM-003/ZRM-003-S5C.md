# 1. Purpose

The Constitutional Invariant Candidate Registry defines the canonical registry for every Constitutional Invariant Candidate extracted during Stage 5.

It establishes:

- the official candidate record specification;
- lifecycle management;
- constitutional traceability;
- dependency tracking;
- registry integrity;
- auditability; and
- readiness for constitutional classification.

The Registry SHALL function as the sole constitutional working ledger for invariant candidates until ratification.

It SHALL introduce no mathematics.

It SHALL preserve only discovered mathematical knowledge.

---

# 2. Constitutional Authority

This document derives constitutional authority from:

- ZRM-003-S5A
- ZRM-003-S5B
- ZRM-003-EP

This registry SHALL NOT:

- create axioms;
- create theorems;
- create invariants;
- modify mathematical meaning.

Its purpose is solely to register extracted candidates for constitutional evaluation.

---

# 3. Constitutional Principles

## Principle 1 — Single Registry

Every extracted candidate SHALL exist in exactly one Constitutional Candidate Registry.

Duplicate registries are prohibited.

---

## Principle 2 — Registration Before Classification

No candidate may enter Classification (S5D) before successful registration.

Registration always precedes classification.

---

## Principle 3 — Immutable History

Registry history SHALL be append-only.

No historical entry may be deleted.

Corrections SHALL create new revisions.

---

## Principle 4 — Complete Traceability

Every candidate SHALL maintain complete provenance to its originating mathematics.

Traceability SHALL never be broken.

---

## Principle 5 — Mathematical Neutrality

The Registry SHALL never alter:

- theorem wording;
- invariant wording;
- proofs;
- predicates;
- constitutional meaning.

It records.

It never authors.

---

## Principle 6 — Constitutional Auditability

Every modification SHALL be permanently recorded.

Every reviewer SHALL be identifiable.

Every decision SHALL be reproducible.

# 5. Candidate Record Specification

Every registry entry SHALL contain the following fields.

## Identity

- Candidate ID
- Version
- Status

---

## Constitutional Statement

Human-readable preservation law.

---

## Mathematical Predicate

Formal mathematical expression.

---

## Verification Predicate

Implementation-independent verification predicate suitable for deterministic validation.

---

## Origin Family

One of:

- Identity
- Event
- Time
- State
- Cross-Family

---

## Scope

One of:

### Family

Applies to a single mathematical family.

### Cross-Family

Applies across multiple mathematical families.

### Foundational

Applies to every Reality Mathematics family.

---

## Origin Theorems

Every originating theorem SHALL be listed.

Example:

```
TH-I-002
TH-E-004
```

---

## Proof References

Every theorem reference SHALL include precise proof provenance.

Minimum acceptable provenance:

- theorem identifier;
- proof section;
- proof step(s).

Example:

```
TH-E-004
Proof
Steps 3–6
```

---

## Extraction Rationale

Why the candidate represents a preservation law.

---

## Dependency Graph

The registry SHALL record:

- theorem dependencies;
- invariant dependencies;
- cross-family dependencies.

---

## Reviewer

Reviewer identity.

---

## Date

Registration timestamp.

---

## Revision History

Complete immutable history.

# 6. Registry Status Model

Candidates SHALL exist in exactly one status.

## Extracted

Produced by S5B.

---

## Registered

Successfully entered into the registry.

---

## Ready for Classification

Registry validation complete.

Eligible for S5D.

---

## Classified

Classification completed.

---

## Ratified

Approved through constitutional ratification.

---

## Frozen

Permanently frozen.

Constitutionally authoritative.

---

## Rejected

Candidate failed constitutional review.

Rejected candidates:

- SHALL remain permanently recorded;
- SHALL NOT proceed further;
- MAY only reappear through an entirely new extraction process.
