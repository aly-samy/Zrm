# Constitutional Invariant Discovery Framework
 
**Document ID:** ZRM-003-S5A **Parent Module:** ZRM-003 — Reality Mathematics **Stage:** Stage 5 — Invariant Workshop **Status:** Constitutional Draft v1.0 (Final) **Authority:** ZRM-003 Constitutional Execution Protocol (ZRM-003-EP)
  
# 1. Purpose
 
This document establishes the constitutional framework governing the discovery of **Constitutional Invariants** within Reality Mathematics.
 
It defines:
 
 
- what an invariant is;
 
- how invariants differ from axioms and theorems;
 
- how invariants are discovered;
 
- how candidate invariants are evaluated;
 
- how admissible invariants enter the constitutional ratification process.
 

 
This document introduces **no new mathematics**.
 
Its purpose is to establish a repeatable constitutional methodology for identifying preservation laws from previously ratified mathematical knowledge.
  
# 2. Constitutional Position
 
Within Reality Mathematics, constitutional knowledge evolves through successive layers.
 `Axioms         ↓ Theorems         ↓ Constitutional Invariants         ↓ Transformations ` 
Each layer has a distinct constitutional responsibility.
 
  
 
Constitutional Artifact
 
Responsibility
 
   
 
Axiom
 
Defines fundamental truth
 
 
 
Theorem
 
Derives mathematical truth
 
 
 
Invariant
 
Preserves mathematical truth
 
 
 
Transformation
 
Operates upon mathematical truth while preserving applicable invariants
 
  
  
# 3. Constitutional Definition
 
A **Constitutional Invariant** is a mathematically proven preservation law that SHALL remain true under every valid constitutional operation permitted by Reality Mathematics.
 
An invariant is not a new mathematical discovery.
 
It is a preservation guarantee extracted from previously ratified mathematical theorems.
 
A constitutional operation is any mathematically valid transformation, composition, evaluation, derivation, or interpretation permitted by Reality Mathematics. The formal taxonomy of operations is introduced in the Transformation Workshop, but their existence is assumed here solely for defining preservation laws.
  
# 4. Relationship to Other Constitutional Artifacts
 
## Axioms
 
Axioms establish the immutable assumptions of Reality Mathematics.
 
They require no proof.
  
## Theorems
 
Theorems formally derive mathematical consequences from axioms and previously ratified theorems.
 
They establish new mathematical knowledge.
  
## Constitutional Invariants
 
Invariants do not derive additional mathematics.
 
They identify mathematical truths that every constitutional operation must preserve.
  
## Transformations
 
Transformations modify constitutional objects while remaining fully compliant with all applicable invariants.
 
No valid transformation may violate a constitutional invariant.
  
# 5. Constitutional Discovery Principles
 
## Principle 1 — Derived Discovery
 
Every Constitutional Invariant SHALL originate from previously ratified mathematical theorems.
 
No invariant may introduce independent mathematical knowledge.
  
## Principle 2 — Preservation
 
An invariant SHALL describe something that remains preserved.
 
It shall never describe a temporary observation, implementation detail, or optimization.
  
## Principle 3 — Cross-Operation Validity
 
Every accepted invariant SHALL remain true regardless of the sequence, composition, or repetition of valid constitutional operations.
  
## Principle 4 — Implementation Independence
 
An invariant SHALL not depend upon:
 
 
- programming languages;
 
- storage systems;
 
- APIs;
 
- serialization formats;
 
- databases;
 
- execution engines;
 
- infrastructure technologies.
 

 
Its validity is purely mathematical.
  
## Principle 5 — Constitutional Universality
 
An accepted invariant SHALL hold for every constitutionally valid implementation of Reality Mathematics.
  
# 6. Constitutional Admissibility Criteria
 
A candidate invariant SHALL satisfy every criterion below.
 
## IC-1 — Mathematical Origin
 
The candidate SHALL originate from one or more ratified theorems.
  
## IC-2 — Preservation Law
 
The candidate SHALL describe a property that must remain preserved.
  
## IC-3 — Constitutional Scope
 
The candidate SHALL remain valid across every constitutionally valid mathematical model.
  
## IC-4 — Non-Redundancy
 
The candidate SHALL introduce a unique preservation guarantee not already represented by another accepted invariant.
  
## IC-5 — Non-Theorem
 
The candidate SHALL not merely restate an existing theorem.
 
Instead, it shall express the preservation property implied by one or more theorems.
  
## IC-6 — Non-Axiom
 
The candidate SHALL not redefine or duplicate an existing constitutional axiom.
  
## IC-7 — Machine Verifiability
 
The candidate SHALL be expressible as a deterministic mathematical verification predicate.
 
This predicate remains implementation-independent.
  
## IC-8 — Constitutional Necessity
 
The candidate SHALL contribute a unique preservation guarantee whose absence would reduce the constitutional completeness of Reality Mathematics.
  
# 7. Extraction Methodology
 
Candidate invariants SHALL be extracted through the following constitutional workflow.
 `Frozen Theorems         ↓ Review Formal Proofs         ↓ Identify Preservation Properties         ↓ Identify Cross-Theorem Constraints         ↓ Generalize Preservation Law         ↓ Remove Implementation Language         ↓ Create Candidate Invariant         ↓ Run Admissibility Review         ↓ Candidate Registry ` 
Every candidate shall retain complete traceability to its originating theorem family.
  
# 8. Required Forms
 
Every accepted invariant SHALL exist in three constitutional forms.
 
## 8.1 Constitutional Statement
 
Human-readable preservation law.
 
Example:
 
 
Constitutional Identity SHALL never be created through derivation.
 
  
## 8.2 Mathematical Predicate
 
Formal mathematical expression.
 
Example:
 `∀ operation createsIdentity(operation) = false `  
## 8.3 Verification Predicate
 
Machine-verifiable mathematical rule.
 
Example:
 `Verify(operation): CreatedIdentityCount = 0 ` 
Verification predicates represent abstract mathematical validation only.
 
They SHALL remain independent of implementation technology.
  
# 9. Classification
 
Accepted candidates SHALL be classified into one of the following categories.
 
## Foundational Invariant
 
Applies to all Reality Mathematics.
 
Must be preserved by every mathematical family, transformation, module, and implementation.
  
## Cross-Family Invariant
 
Applies across multiple mathematical families.
  
## Family Invariant
 
Applies exclusively within a single mathematical family.
  
## Derived Candidate
 
Requires additional constitutional analysis before ratification.
  
## Rejected Candidate
 
Fails one or more admissibility criteria.
  
# 10. Constitutional Provenance
 
Every candidate invariant SHALL maintain complete constitutional traceability.
 
The provenance record SHALL include:

This provenance enables complete constitutional traceability throughout the Constitutional Rejection Cascade defined by ZRM-003-EP.

No invariant may bypass any stage of this workflow.
