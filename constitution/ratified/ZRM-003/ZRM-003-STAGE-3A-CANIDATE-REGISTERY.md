# 1. Constitutional Purpose

The Candidate Registry records all candidate mathematical theorems discovered through the constitutional process defined by **ZRM-003-STAGE-3A**.

The Registry exists to organize, review, refine, and govern candidate theorems prior to formal proof.

The Registry is **not** constitutional authority.

Constitutional authority resides exclusively in the frozen discovery process defined by **ZRM-003-STAGE-3A**.

---

# 2. Constitutional Authority

This Registry derives its authority exclusively from:

- ZRM-001 — Constitutional Foundation
- ZRM-002 — Constitutional Axiom System
- ZRM-003-STAGE-3A — Constitutional Theorem Discovery

The Registry SHALL NOT:

- introduce new axioms;
- modify Constitutional Vocabulary;
- redefine Constitutional Axioms;
- establish mathematical truth.

Its sole purpose is to record discovery outputs.

---

# 3. Registry Governance

The Candidate Registry is a **living working artifact**.

Throughout Stage 3A, candidate theorems may be:

- added;
- revised;
- merged;
- split;
- reclassified;
- disqualified;
- removed.

No modification to this Registry alters the Constitution.

Only the frozen constitutional process governs discovery.

---

# 4. Registry Lifecycle

Every Candidate Theorem SHALL progress through the following lifecycle.

```text
Hypothesis
      │
      ▼
Discovery Review
      │
      ▼
Discovery Gates Passed
      │
      ▼
Proof Eligible
      │
      ▼
Under Proof (Stage 3B)
      │
      ▼
Ratified
      │
      ▼
Frozen
```

No theorem may bypass this lifecycle.

---

# 5. Registry Status Definitions

## Hypothesis

The theorem has been identified but has not yet completed discovery review.

---

## Discovery Review

The theorem is undergoing constitutional evaluation against the Discovery Gates.

---

## Discovery Gates Passed

The theorem has satisfied DG-1 through DG-9 and is eligible for Council consideration.

---

## Proof Eligible

The theorem has:

- passed every Discovery Gate;
- been accepted into the Registry;
- received Council approval for proof;
- satisfied all Stage 3A exit requirements applicable to the candidate.

Only Proof Eligible candidates may enter Stage 3B.

---

## Under Proof

The theorem is currently undergoing formal proof during Stage 3B.

---

## Ratified

The theorem has successfully completed formal proof and Council ratification.

---

## Frozen

The theorem has become part of the Constitutional Theorem Set and is immutable.

---

## Disqualified

The candidate has been removed because it:

- duplicates another theorem;
- is a corollary;
- is an implementation concern;
- is a definition;
- is unsupported by discovery;
- or fails constitutional review.

Disqualified entries remain in Registry history for auditability.

---

# 6. Candidate Theorem Record Format

Every registry entry SHALL contain the following fields.

| Field | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| ID | Temporary theorem identifier (TH-XXX) |
| Name | Canonical theorem name |
| Statement | Candidate mathematical statement |
| Intuition | Why the theorem appears true |
| Related Witnesses | Supporting Constitutional Witnesses |
| Hypothesized Dependencies | Candidate supporting axioms |
| Confidence | Low / Medium / High |
| Discovery Rationale | Discovery summary |
| Discovery Gate Result | DG-1 through DG-9 outcome |
| Review History | Council decisions |
| Status | Current lifecycle status |

Candidate theorems SHOULD be grouped by constitutional domain where appropriate.

Recommended organization:

1. Identity Mathematics

6. Other Constitutional Mathematics

---

# 8. Registry Maintenance Rules

The Registry SHALL maintain complete revision history.

Every modification SHALL preserve:

- theorem identifier;
- review history;
- rationale for changes;
- Council decisions.

Merged or split theorems SHALL reference their originating identifiers.

Disqualified candidates SHALL remain recorded for constitutional auditability.

---

# 9. Registry Integrity Principles

The Registry SHALL preserve:

- traceability;
- transparency;
- reproducibility;
- auditability;
- constitutional consistency.

The Registry SHALL NOT become a source of constitutional authority.

It records discovery.

It does not define discovery.

---

# 10. Initial Registry State

At initialization:

- No candidate theorem has been accepted.
- No candidate theorem has been proven.
- No candidate theorem has been ratified.
- No candidate theorem has been frozen.

The Registry is intentionally empty.

Population SHALL occur through successive constitutional discovery rounds governed by **ZRM-003-STAGE-3A**.
