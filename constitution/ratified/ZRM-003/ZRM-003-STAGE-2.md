# ZRM-003 — Stage 2: Constitutional Axiom Workshop

**Document ID:** ZRM-003-STAGE-2  
**Version:** 1.0 (Frozen)  
**Status:** Frozen Constitutional Process  
**Authority:** ZRM-001, ZRM-002, Constitutional Benchmark Suite (Stage 0), Constitutional Vocabulary (Stage 1)

---

# 1. Purpose

Stage 2 establishes the constitutional process for identifying, validating, and ratifying the irreducible mathematical axioms of the Zyppi Reality Model (ZRM).

It does **not** define the axioms themselves.

It defines **how constitutional axioms are admitted into the model.**

No theorem, mathematical proof, implementation, algorithm, or software behavior shall be introduced in this stage.

---

# 2. Constitutional Question

Stage 2 answers exactly one constitutional question:

> **Which mathematical statements are universally true within the Zyppi Reality Model?**

No additional constitutional question shall be answered by this stage.

---

# 3. Inputs

Stage 2 depends exclusively upon:

- Stage 0 — Constitutional Benchmark Suite
- Stage 1 — Constitutional Mathematical Vocabulary

No other sources are constitutional inputs.

---

# 4. Outputs

Stage 2 produces:

- Constitutional Axiom Registry
- Ratified Constitutional Axioms (AX-XXX)

These become the exclusive foundation for Stage 3.

---

# 5. Principle of Strict Minimality

The constitutional axiom set SHALL contain **exactly the number of axioms necessary** to model every Constitutional Witness.

No numerical target shall exist.

A new axiom may be admitted only if all of the following are true:

1. It cannot be derived from existing axioms.
2. Its removal makes at least one Constitutional Witness impossible or materially incomplete to model.
3. No existing axiom can be expanded without violating Atomic Independence.
4. Construction Validation demonstrates that the axiom is genuinely required.

---

# 6. Constitutional Axiom Requirements

Every proposed axiom SHALL satisfy all requirements.

---

## AR-1 — Irreducibility

The statement SHALL not be derivable from existing constitutional axioms.

---

## AR-2 — Witness Applicability

The statement SHALL support at least one Constitutional Witness.

---

## AR-3 — Non-Contradiction

The statement SHALL not contradict any higher constitutional authority.

---

## AR-4 — Vocabulary Compliance

Only Stage 1 constitutional vocabulary may be used.

---

## AR-5 — Technology Independence

The statement SHALL contain no technology assumptions.

---

## AR-6 — Implementation Independence

The statement SHALL contain no implementation assumptions.

---

## AR-7 — Universality

The statement SHALL always be true within the constitutional domain of the ZRM.

---

## AR-8 — Necessity

Removing the statement SHALL make one or more Constitutional Witnesses impossible or materially incomplete to model.

Necessity shall be evaluated both:

- individually; and
- collectively through Construction Validation.

---

## AR-9 — Atomic Independence

An axiom violates this requirement **only if** it can be mathematically separated into two or more independent axioms that each independently satisfy Witness Applicability.

Grammatical conjunctions do **not** constitute a violation if the underlying mathematical truth remains singular.

---

# 7. Prior Authority Check

Before proposing a new axiom, reviewers SHALL verify that the proposed statement is not already:

- explicitly defined in ZRM-001;
- explicitly defined in ZRM-002;
- implied by higher constitutional authority;
- derivable from existing constitutional axioms.

If any condition succeeds, the proposal SHALL NOT become a new constitutional axiom.

# 10. Constitutional Axiom Registry

The registry records only ratified constitutional axioms.

Each entry SHALL possess:

- immutable identifier;
- immutable statement;
- immutable review history.

Existing axioms SHALL never be silently modified.

Any change requires a constitutional amendment.

---

# 11. Validation by Construction

Passing constitutional review **does not** freeze the Constitutional Axiom Registry.

Instead, the Registry enters **Candidate Status**.

Construction Validation exists to determine whether the candidate axiom set is sufficient to support theorem construction.

Construction Validation SHALL include pilot theorem drafting that deliberately exercises multiple constitutional domains simultaneously (for example Identity, Time, Event, and Causality).

The purpose is to discover missing foundational truths before constitutional freeze.

Construction Validation SHALL verify:

- sufficiency of the candidate axiom set;
- absence of hidden assumptions;
- absence of missing primitives;
- cross-domain consistency;
- ability to support theorem construction.

Construction Validation SHALL NOT produce ratified constitutional theorems.

Pilot theorems are validation artifacts only.

If Construction Validation discovers missing constitutional primitives, the Registry returns to Draft status and new candidate axioms enter the full constitutional review process beginning with AG-1.

No shortcut admission path exists.

# 13. Exit Criteria

The Constitutional Axiom Registry may advance from Candidate to Frozen only when all conditions are satisfied.

- Every candidate axiom passes AG-1 through AG-10.
- Every candidate satisfies Strict Minimality.
- Every candidate survives Prior Authority Check.
- Every candidate survives adversarial review.
- Construction Validation succeeds.
- No missing constitutional primitive is discovered.
- Every Constitutional Witness possesses sufficient axiomatic foundations for theorem construction.

---

# 14. Freeze Conditions

After freezing:

- no ratified axiom may be modified;
- no ratified axiom may be deleted;
- new axioms require constitutional amendment;
- historical registry versions remain immutable.
