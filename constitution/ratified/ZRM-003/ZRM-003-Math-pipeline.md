# One refinement before freezing
I would rename Stage 5 from "Constitutional Algebra" to Constitutional Transformations.
"Algebra" is mathematically precise but narrow. 

Your framework will likely include:
- operators
- mappings
- projections
- compositions
- morphisms
- transformations
- equivalence relations

"Constitutional Transformations" naturally encompasses all of these, making it a more future-proof stage without constraining the mathematical formalism.

With this roadmap frozen, the project gains a stable constitutional spine, after which you can confidently begin Stage 3A: Constitutional Theorem Discovery.
