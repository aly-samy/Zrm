## ZRM-003-STAGE-3A
### Formalization of the Stage 3A Internal Execution Pipeline

**Amendment ID:** ZRM-003-STAGE-3A-AMD-001

**Status:** Proposed

**Authority:**
- ZRM-001 — Constitutional Foundation
- ZRM-002 — Constitutional Axiom System

# Purpose

This amendment clarifies the internal execution model of Stage 3A.

The Council determined that the recent Identity Mathematics review exposed an implicit architectural phase that had never been formally defined.

The issue was not a weakness in the Discovery Gates.

The issue was the absence of an explicit **Mathematical Classification** phase between discovery and registry recommendation.

This amendment formalizes that phase.

---

# Constitutional Finding

During Round 1, the Council observed repeated **category leakage**.

Candidate theorems concerning Events, Subjects, Objects, State, and Evidence entered Identity Mathematics because ownership classification occurred implicitly rather than explicitly.

This led to proposals for:

- DG-0 (Domain Ownership)
- Trans-Constitutional Mathematics
- Bridge Registries

After constitutional review, the Council concluded that these proposals attempted to solve a workflow problem by modifying the constitutional discovery process.

The underlying problem was not insufficient Discovery Gates.

The underlying problem was the absence of a dedicated Classification phase.

---

# Constitutional Decision

The Council adopts a four-phase execution model within Stage 3A.

Discovery and Classification are separate constitutional activities.

---

```
Hypothesis Discovery
        │
        ▼

        │
        ▼

```

---

# Phase 1 — Hypothesis Discovery

Purpose:

Identify possible mathematical truths emerging from the Constitutional Axioms.

Activities include:

- observing recurring mathematical behavior;
- identifying candidate consequences;
- discovering cross-axiom interactions;
- comparing Constitutional Witnesses.

No ownership is assigned.

No theorem is rejected.

No theorem is promoted.

Output:

Candidate Hypotheses.

---

# Phase 2 — Mathematical Classification

Purpose:

Assign each hypothesis to exactly one mathematical family.

Examples:

- Identity Mathematics
- Event Mathematics
- State Mathematics
- Temporal Mathematics
- Evidence Mathematics
- Projection Mathematics
- Transformation Mathematics

Classification answers one question only:

> What mathematical object is primarily constrained by this theorem?

A theorem SHALL possess exactly one primary mathematical family.

Dependencies on other families are permitted.

Multiple ownership is prohibited.

---

# Phase 3 — Family Purification

Purpose:

Reduce the selected mathematical family to its smallest irreducible theorem set.

Reviewers SHALL:

- remove category leakage;
- merge duplicates;
- split compound theorems;
- identify hidden corollaries;
- discover missing candidates;
- challenge every theorem through adversarial review.

The objective is mathematical minimality.

Not theorem quantity.

---

# Phase 4 — Registry Recommendation

Only purified candidates proceed to Registry evaluation.

Candidates then receive Discovery Gate evaluation.

Possible Registry recommendations include:

- Hypothesis
- Discovery Review
- Discovery Gates Passed
- Proof Eligible
- Disqualified

No theorem shall receive Proof Eligible status before completion of all previous phases.

---

# Round Scope Declaration

Every discovery round SHALL begin with a Round Scope Declaration.

Template:

```text
Primary Mathematical Family:
[Selected Family]

Secondary Dependencies:
[List or None]

Excluded Families:
[List]
```

Example:

```text
Primary Mathematical Family:
Identity Mathematics

Secondary Dependencies:
None

Excluded Families:

• Event Mathematics
• State Mathematics
• Evidence Mathematics
• Projection Mathematics
• Transformation Mathematics
• Subject Mathematics
• Object Mathematics
```

Round Scope governs workflow.

It is not a constitutional gate.

---

# DP-9 — Mathematical Isolation

The Council adopts the following Discovery Principle.

**DP-9 — Mathematical Isolation**

> When evaluating a candidate within a mathematical family, reviewers should mentally remove every other mathematical family.

If the theorem no longer primarily constrains the selected family, it SHALL be reassigned to the appropriate discovery round.

DP-9 is guidance.

It is not a Discovery Gate.

---

# Theorem Ownership

Every theorem SHALL have:

- exactly one primary mathematical family;
- zero or more dependencies.

Example:

```text
Owner Family:
Identity Mathematics

Dependencies:

• AX-002 Identity
• AX-005 Valid Time
• Future Temporal Theorems
```

Singular ownership preserves:

- deterministic proof ordering;
- unambiguous dependency graphs;
- clear constitutional responsibility.

---

# Dependency Management

The Council rejects the introduction of:

- Trans-Constitutional Mathematics;
- Bridge Registry;
- Dependency Mapping Index (DMI).

Existing theorem metadata already includes:

> Hypothesized Dependencies

This field is sufficient for Stage 3A.

Formal dependency validation belongs to Stage 3B.

---

# Constitutional Principles

The Council reaffirms:

- Discovery discovers.
- Classification assigns ownership.
- Purification removes category leakage.
- Discovery Gates evaluate maturity.
- Registry records candidates.
- Stage 3B proves mathematics.

Each phase serves one constitutional purpose.

No phase shall assume the responsibilities of another.

---

# Constitutional Resolution

The Council concludes that the correct solution to category leakage is not additional Discovery Gates.

The correct solution is explicit separation between:

- Discovery;
- Classification;
- Purification;
- Registry Recommendation.

This preserves the simplicity of Stage 3A while ensuring deterministic ownership, minimal theorem sets, and a stable foundation for Stage 3B.

---

# Final Resolution

**Status:** APPROVED

This amendment is adopted as the standard execution model for all future Stage 3A discovery rounds.

Effective immediately, every mathematical discovery round SHALL follow the execution pipeline defined in this amendment.
