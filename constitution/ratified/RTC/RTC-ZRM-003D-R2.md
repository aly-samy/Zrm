# RTC-ZRM-003D-R2
## State Mathematics — Ratification Framework

**Document ID:** RTC-ZRM-003D-R2  
**Family:** ZRM-003D — State Mathematics  
**Status:** Ratification Framework  
**Classification:** Constitutional Process  
**Authority:** Zyppi Constitutional Council

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the constitutional process used to determine which retained State Mathematics candidates become permanent mathematical theorems.

No theorem gains constitutional authority merely because it survived discovery.

Every retained candidate SHALL undergo independent mathematical proof.

Only proven theorems SHALL enter the Constitutional Registry.

---

**RTC-ZRM-003D-R1A — Classification & Purification**

- TH-S001 — State Derivation

- TH-S004 — State Composition
- TH-S006 — State Identity Preservation
- TH-S007 — State Completeness
- TH-S011 — State Independence

- TH-S014 — State Temporal Consistency

---

# 3. Proof Independence

Each theorem SHALL be proven independently.

No theorem may inherit constitutional authority from another without explicit mathematical proof.

Every proof SHALL stand on its own.

---

# 4. Required Proof Structure

Every proof SHALL contain the following sections.

---

## A. Candidate Statement

Formal statement of the proposed theorem.

---

## B. Mathematical Context

Identify:

- prerequisite constitutional families
- prerequisite definitions
- dependency graph

---

## C. Formal Proof

Provide a complete mathematical proof.

The proof SHALL demonstrate that the theorem follows necessarily from constitutional mathematics.

---

## D. Counterexample Analysis

Attempt to falsify the theorem.

The proof SHALL demonstrate why every counterexample fails.

---

## E. Independence Test

Demonstrate that the theorem cannot be completely derived from previously ratified theorems.

If derivable, the theorem SHALL become a corollary.

---

## F. Discovery Gate Validation

Validate:

- DG-1 through DG-9

---

## G. Constitutional Decision

Exactly one outcome SHALL be assigned.

- Ratify
- Merge
- Reclassify
- Corollary
- Reject

# 6. Corollary Resolution

Candidates marked for purification SHALL remain suspended until sufficient primary theorems have been proven.

Suspended candidates:

- TH-S002
- TH-S005
- TH-S008
- TH-S010
- TH-S012
- TH-S015
- TH-S016

After primary proofs are complete, each suspended candidate SHALL be evaluated as one of:

- Independent theorem
- Corollary
- Merge
- Rejection

---

# 7. Constitutional Success Criteria

A theorem SHALL be ratified only if it satisfies all of the following:

- mathematically valid
- constitutionally necessary
- independent
- technology neutral
- implementation independent
- non-redundant
- non-semantic
- consistent with Identity, Event and Time Mathematics

Failure of any requirement SHALL prevent ratification.

---

# 8. Registry Rules

Only ratified theorems SHALL receive permanent constitutional identifiers.

Rejected candidates SHALL never enter the constitutional registry.

Merged candidates SHALL inherit the status of the theorem into which they merge.

Corollaries SHALL be recorded separately and SHALL NOT become constitutional axioms.
