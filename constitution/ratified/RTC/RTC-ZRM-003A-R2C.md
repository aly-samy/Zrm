# 5. PG-1 — Formal Proof

Let

\[
f:C\rightarrow I
\]

be the identity mapping established by TH-003.

Assume identity transfer is possible.

There exist two distinct constituents

\[
a,b\in C,\qquad a\neq b
\]

such that

\[
f(a)\rightarrow b.
\]

After transfer,

\[
f(b)=f(a).
\]

From TH-003,

\[
f(x)=f(y)\iff x=y.
\]

Therefore,

\[
a=b.
\]

This contradicts the original assumption

\[
a\neq b.
\]

Hence,

identity transfer is impossible.

Therefore,

Identity cannot be transferred between constitutional constituents.
