# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the constitutional framework for **Identity Mathematics**, the first mathematical family within the Reality Mathematics architecture.

Identity Mathematics defines the mathematical principles governing constitutional identity. It provides the foundation upon which every subsequent Reality Mathematics family is built.

This document governs the discovery, validation, and ratification process of Identity Mathematics. It does not define the mathematical theorems themselves.

---

# 2. Constitutional Question

Identity Mathematics answers one constitutional question:

> **What mathematical laws govern the existence, uniqueness, and continuity of constitutional identities?**

Every theorem within this family SHALL contribute directly to answering this question.

---

# 3. Scope

Identity Mathematics includes the study of:

- Identity existence
- Identity uniqueness
- Identity continuity
- Identity persistence
- Identity distinguishability
- Identity equivalence
- Identity composition
- Identity independence

The purpose is to discover the irreducible mathematical truths governing Identity.

---

# 4. Out of Scope

Identity Mathematics SHALL NOT define or govern:

- Events
- Time
- State
- Causality
- Execution
- Storage
- Databases
- Networking
- Permissions
- Security
- Representations
- User interfaces
- Programming languages
- Runtime behavior
- Implementation strategies

These belong to other constitutional families.

---

# 5. Constitutional Position

Identity Mathematics is the foundational mathematical family within Reality Mathematics.

```
Reality Mathematics
│
├── RTC-ZRM-003A — Identity Mathematics
├── RTC-ZRM-003B — Event Mathematics
├── RTC-ZRM-003C — Time Mathematics
└── RTC-ZRM-003D — State Mathematics
```

Identity Mathematics has no prerequisite mathematical family.

Every subsequent Reality Mathematics family SHALL treat Identity Mathematics as constitutional authority.

---

# 7. Constitutional Constraints

Every theorem proposed within Identity Mathematics SHALL satisfy the following principles:

### IM-1 — Mathematical Purity

Theorems SHALL describe mathematical truths only.

---

### IM-2 — Constitutional Independence

Identity Mathematics SHALL not depend upon Event, Time, or State Mathematics.

---

### IM-3 — Technology Neutrality

Theorems SHALL remain valid regardless of implementation technology.

---

### IM-4 — Observer Independence

Identity SHALL exist independently of observers, representations, or interpretations.

---

### IM-5 — Family Separation

Identity Mathematics SHALL not define concepts belonging to other mathematical families.

---

# 8. Discovery Objectives

The Identity Mathematics Registry SHALL identify the smallest possible set of irreducible mathematical theorems governing constitutional identity.

The discovery process SHALL eliminate:

- Definitions
- Corollaries
- Implementation details
- Optimization strategies
- Technology assumptions
- Semantic interpretations
- Redundant mathematical truths

Only fundamental constitutional theorems may enter the frozen registry.

---

# 9. Constitutional Authority

Upon completion of Registry Freeze (R3), the Identity Mathematics Registry SHALL become the sole constitutional authority governing Identity within Reality Mathematics.

All future constitutional documents SHALL reference the frozen registry rather than redefining Identity.

---

# Constitutional Principle

> **Identity is the mathematical foundation of Reality. Every constitutional object must first possess Identity before it may participate in Events, Time, or State.**
