# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the constitutional framework for discovering the fundamental mathematical laws governing **Time** within the Zyppi Constitution.

Time Mathematics defines the mathematical structure required to reason about temporal relationships between constitutional Events.

It does **not** define clocks, timestamps, synchronization protocols, storage mechanisms, scheduling algorithms, or implementation strategies.

Its sole purpose is discovering timeless mathematical truths regarding temporal structure.

# 8. Expected Discovery Domains

The Council anticipates discovery within, but not limited to, the following mathematical domains.

---

## Temporal Ordering

Mathematical relationships defining the ordering of Events within logical time.

---

## Temporal Precedence

Conditions under which one Event must precede another.

---

## Causality

The mathematical relation establishing when one Event is a necessary logical prerequisite for another.

Time Mathematics SHALL discover causal precedence independently of chronological timestamps.

---

## Concurrency

Mathematical conditions under which two Events possess no causal dependency.

Formally, neither Event precedes the other in the logical ordering relation.

Concurrency is **not** simultaneity.

---

## Partial Ordering

Temporal structures in which only some Events possess ordering relationships.

---

## Temporal Independence

Mathematical independence of temporal paths.

---

## Temporal Consistency

Consistency rules governing temporal relationships.

---

## Temporal Topology

The mathematical shape of constitutional time.

---

## Temporal Composition

Composition of temporal relations into larger temporal structures.

---

## Temporal Equivalence

Conditions under which distinct temporal descriptions represent identical logical ordering.

---

## Observer Mapping

Mathematical mappings between observer-specific chronological measurements and observer-independent logical time.

Observer Mapping SHALL NOT redefine the constitutional concept of Observer established by Identity Mathematics.
