# 5. PG-1 — Formal Proof

Let

- \(C\) be the set of constitutional constituents.
- \(I(c)\) denote the identity of constituent \(c\).
- \(Rep(c)\) denote the set of all representations of \(c\).

Assume every representation is removed.

\[
Rep(c)=\varnothing
\]

Suppose this changes identity.

Then

\[
I_{before}(c)\neq I_{after}(c)
\]

However, Identity Immutability (TH-003) states

\[
I_{before}(c)=I_{after}(c)
\]

Therefore a contradiction exists.

Likewise,

adding,

changing,

or deleting any representation cannot alter identity.

Therefore

identity is independent of representations.
