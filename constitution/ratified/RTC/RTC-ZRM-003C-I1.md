# 3. Interpretation Principle

Although the Time Mathematics Registry contains five independently ratified theorems, those theorems SHALL be interpreted as describing a **single unified mathematical structure**.

The constitutional object governed by Time Mathematics is the logical temporal relation over constitutional Events.

The individual theorems describe different mathematical properties of that single object.

---

# 4. Unified Mathematical Structure

Let

\[
T=(E,\prec)
\]

where:

- \(E\) is the constitutional Event set.
- \(\prec\) is constitutional logical precedence.

The ratified Time Mathematics Registry SHALL be interpreted as establishing this single mathematical structure.

The five ratified theorems collectively characterize the properties of \(T\).

---

# 5. Interpretation of Ratified Theorems

The ratified theorems SHALL be understood as follows.

| Theorem | Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|
| TH-T001 — Logical Time Ordering | Establishes the existence of constitutional logical precedence. |
| TH-T002 — Concurrency | Defines the absence of logical precedence between independent Events. |
| TH-T003 — Partial Ordering | Defines the governing mathematical relation over constitutional Events. |
| TH-T004 — Temporal Topology | Describes the structural form induced by the partial order. |
| TH-T005 — Temporal Irreversibility | Describes the directional property of the partial order. |

None of these theorems establish separate mathematical universes.

They describe different aspects of one constitutional structure.

---

# 6. Implementation Interpretation

Future constitutional implementations SHALL NOT interpret the five theorems as requiring five independent validation systems.

Instead:

> Validation of the constitutional strict partial order SHALL be understood to satisfy the constitutional requirements established by TH-T001 through TH-T005.

This interpretation exists solely to prevent unnecessary duplication of implementation logic.
