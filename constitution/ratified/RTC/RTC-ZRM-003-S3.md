# 7. Dependency Rules

Every constitutional family SHALL satisfy the following rules.

## DR-1 — Single Ownership

Every mathematical family SHALL have exactly one constitutional owner.

Duplicate ownership is prohibited.

---

## DR-2 — Acyclic Dependencies

Constitutional dependencies SHALL form a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG).

Circular authority is prohibited.

---

## DR-3 — Upstream Authority

Families may depend only upon constitutionally earlier families.

Authority never flows upward.

---

## DR-4 — No Cross-Layer Leakage

Implementation concepts SHALL NOT appear within mathematical families.

Likewise, mathematical families SHALL NOT depend upon implementation concerns.

---

## DR-5 — Constitutional Stability

Once ratified, dependency direction SHALL remain immutable except through constitutional amendment.
