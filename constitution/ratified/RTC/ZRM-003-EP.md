# 4. Constitutional Principles

## Principle I — Forward Progress

Work SHALL move only through the defined execution stages.

Progression is strictly sequential.

---

## Principle II — Exclusive Stage Ownership

Each stage owns exactly one constitutional responsibility.

| Stage | Owns |
|--------|----------------------------|
|3|Discovery|
|4|Theorem creation|
|5|Invariant creation|
|6|Adversarial testing|
|7|Benchmark validation|
|8|Module drafting|
|9|Integration verification|
|10|Final ratification|

No stage may perform another stage's responsibility.

---

## Principle III — Read-Only Validation

Stages 6, 7 and 9 SHALL be observational only.

These stages may:

- execute tests
- discover failures
- produce reports
- identify contradictions

These stages SHALL NOT:

- modify mathematics
- rewrite theorems
- create invariants
- edit constitutional documents

Validation never performs authorship.

---

## Principle IV — Constitutional Rejection Cascade

When a failure is discovered, correction SHALL occur at the earliest stage responsible for the defect.

The validation stages never repair artifacts.

They only identify defects.

Repairs always occur upstream.

---

### Stage Failure Routing

Failure Type → Return Stage

Candidate defect → Stage 3

Incorrect theorem → Stage 4

Invalid invariant → Stage 5

Drafting error → Stage 8

Integration conflict → Stage 8 or Stage 9

Only the owning stage may modify its artifacts.

---

### Root Cause Escalation

If investigation determines that a defect originated earlier than initially believed, the artifact SHALL return to the earliest responsible stage.

Example:

Invariant Failure

↓

Root Cause = Incorrect Theorem

↓

Return to Stage 4

Example:

Theorem Failure

↓

Root Cause = Invalid Candidate

↓

Return to Stage 3

Corrections SHALL always occur at the root cause.

---

### Dependency Revalidation

Whenever a theorem, invariant, or candidate is revised, every downstream artifact that depends upon it SHALL automatically re-enter the validation pipeline.

No downstream artifact may retain frozen status after one of its dependencies changes.

---

### Validation Consensus

A rejection SHALL NOT be initiated from a single unverified observation.

A constitutional failure must be supported by:

- formal mathematical proof,
- reproducible validation evidence, or
- Constitutional Council consensus.

Only validated failures may activate the Constitutional Rejection Cascade.

---

### Rejection Circuit Breaker

If an artifact repeatedly fails validation without convergence, the Constitutional Council SHALL review the artifact.

The Council may:

- continue refinement,
- deprecate the artifact,
- return it to an earlier stage,
- reopen candidate discovery.

This prevents infinite refinement cycles while preserving mathematical rigor.

---

## Principle V — Progressive Constitutional Freeze

Authority increases throughout execution.

| Stage | Freeze Status |
|--------|-------------------------|
|3|Candidate (Provisional)|
|4|Theorem (Working)|
|5|Invariant (Candidate Freeze)|
|6|Validation Reports|
|7|Benchmark Reports|
|8|Draft Modules|
|9|Integrated Draft|
|10|Permanent Constitutional Freeze|

Only Stage 10 produces immutable constitutional artifacts.

All earlier freezes remain provisional and may be reopened through the Constitutional Rejection Cascade.
