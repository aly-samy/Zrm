# 5. Proof

From TH-T003, logical time forms a strict partial order.

A strict partial order is asymmetric.

Therefore,

\[
e_1 \prec e_2
\Rightarrow
\neg(e_2 \prec e_1)
\]

Assume the opposite.

Suppose both

\[
e_1 \prec e_2
\]

and

\[
e_2 \prec e_1
\]

are simultaneously true.

This violates asymmetry and creates a logical contradiction.

Consequently, constitutional precedence cannot reverse.

Therefore logical time possesses an immutable direction.

Hence Temporal Irreversibility necessarily follows from constitutional logical ordering.

Q.E.D.
