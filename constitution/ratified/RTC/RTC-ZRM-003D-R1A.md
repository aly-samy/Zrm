# 1. Purpose

This document classifies every candidate discovered during RTC-ZRM-003D-R1 and determines whether each candidate SHALL:

- proceed to formal proof,
- merge into another theorem,
- become a corollary,
- be reassigned to another mathematical family,
- or be rejected.

The objective is to isolate the smallest irreducible mathematical foundation of State Mathematics.

---

# 2. Purification Principles

Every candidate SHALL satisfy the Discovery Gate (DG-1 through DG-9).

Additionally, every candidate SHALL satisfy:

## PG-1 — Irreducibility

The candidate cannot be completely derived from another ratified theorem.

---

## PG-2 — Independence

The candidate expresses a unique mathematical property.

It SHALL NOT merely restate another theorem.

---

## PG-3 — Constitutional Scope

The candidate SHALL describe State Mathematics only.

It SHALL NOT define:

- Projection Mathematics
- Representation Mathematics
- Storage Mathematics
- Execution Mathematics
- Optimization Mathematics

---

## PG-4 — Mathematical Necessity

Removing the candidate must eliminate a genuine mathematical truth.

If nothing fundamental is lost, the candidate SHALL be rejected.
