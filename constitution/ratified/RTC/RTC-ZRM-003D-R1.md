## TH-S001 — State Derivation

**Hypothesis**

Every constitutional State is uniquely derived from:

- constitutional Identity
- constitutionally valid Events
- constitutionally valid Time ordering

State is never primitive.

---

## TH-S002 — State Determinism

**Hypothesis**

Identical constitutional inputs SHALL always derive identical constitutional States.

---

## TH-S003 — State Equivalence

**Hypothesis**

## TH-S004 — State Composition

**Hypothesis**

Complex constitutional States may be composed from smaller constitutional States while preserving constitutional truth.

---

## TH-S005 — State Consistency

**Hypothesis**

A constitutional State cannot contain internally contradictory mathematical facts.

---

## TH-S006 — State Identity Preservation

**Hypothesis**

State evolution SHALL never alter constitutional Identity.

Identity persists independently of State.

## TH-S007 — State Completeness

**Hypothesis**

---

## TH-S008 — State Reachability

**Hypothesis**

## TH-S010 — State Minimality

**Hypothesis**

No constitutional State contains mathematically unnecessary information.

Every retained fact contributes to constitutional truth.

---

## TH-S011 — State Independence

**Hypothesis**

Independent constitutional States may evolve without introducing mathematical dependency.

---

## TH-S012 — State Evolution

**Hypothesis**

State changes occur exclusively through constitutionally ordered Events.

No State transition exists without constitutional Event history.

---

## TH-S013 — State-Event Correspondence

**Hypothesis**

Constitutional State and constitutional Event history possess a mathematically consistent correspondence.

Equivalent histories SHALL produce equivalent constitutional State.

---

## TH-S014 — State Temporal Consistency

**Hypothesis**

Every constitutional State SHALL remain mathematically consistent with constitutional Time ordering.

State SHALL never violate temporal ordering established by ZRM-005.

---

## TH-S015 — State Purity

**Hypothesis**

Constitutional State is determined solely by constitutional inputs.

External effects cannot influence mathematical State.

---

## TH-S016 — State Canonical Form

**Hypothesis**

Equivalent constitutional States possess a unique canonical mathematical representation independent of implementation.

---

## TH-S017 — Projection Completeness

**Hypothesis**
