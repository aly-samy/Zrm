# 1. Purpose

This document performs the constitutional purification of the Event Mathematics candidate registry.

Its purpose is to determine:

- which candidates genuinely belong to Event Mathematics,
- which candidates belong to other mathematical families,
- which candidates are merely corollaries,
- which candidates are implementation patterns,
- which candidates should be deferred until dependent mathematical families are ratified.

No theorem is formally proven during this stage.

No theorem is ratified during this stage.

---

# 2. Constitutional Question

Stage 3A Purification answers one question:

> **Which discovered candidates are genuine Event Mathematics theorems?**

---

# 3. Purification Principles

Every candidate SHALL be evaluated against the following constitutional principles.

## PP-1 — Category Purity

Every theorem belongs to exactly one mathematical family.

No theorem may simultaneously belong to Event Mathematics and another family.

---

## PP-2 — Structural Independence

The theorem must express an independent mathematical truth.

Derived statements SHALL NOT become constitutional theorems.

---

## PP-3 — Implementation Neutrality

Implementation patterns, execution mechanisms and software techniques SHALL NOT become mathematics.

---

## PP-4 — Dependency Isolation

A theorem depending on an unratified mathematical family SHALL be deferred until that family becomes constitutional authority.

---

## PP-5 — Constitutional Minimality

Only irreducible mathematical truths survive purification.
