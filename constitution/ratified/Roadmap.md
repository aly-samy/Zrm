# 1. Executive Summary
The Zyppi Reality Model (ZRM) has undergone significant architectural evolution during Batch-01.

The original objective was to establish the semantic foundation required to support:
- Entity Modeling
- Relationship Modeling
- URL Architecture
- Schema Architecture
- AI Authority Architecture
- Ownership Mapping

As the work evolved, substantial governance, authority, temporal, intelligence, and graph architecture capabilities were added.

The Constitutional Integrity Audit (CIA-01) determined that the foundation is substantially complete but not yet frozen.

This roadmap defines the official path from constitutional architecture toward registry population and implementation.

# 11. Governance Rule
**The project SHALL prioritize:**
- Constitutional Stability
- Registry Readiness
- Registry Population
- Semantic Publication
- Technical Serialization

Implementation concerns SHALL NOT drive constitutional architecture decisions.

Architecture must be frozen before implementation models are designed.

# 12. Success Criteria
ZRM Foundation shall be considered complete only when:
- Foundation Freeze Certification is approved
- CIA-02 passes
- WS-03E passes
- WS-05 Master Registry is complete

At that point the constitutional layer is considered frozen and implementation architecture may begin.
