## 1. Constitutional Purpose
 
ZRM-002 establishes the constitutional ontology through which Reality is represented.
 
It defines the fundamental ontological constructs that allow Reality to be modeled consistently without introducing implementation, execution, business, mathematical, or semantic projection concerns.
 
Its purpose is to answer one constitutional question:
 
 
**"What exists within the constitutional model of Reality, and how may those existents be structurally described?"**

1. North Star
 
2. Founding Principles

## 3. Constitutional Scope
 
ZRM-002 MAY define:
 
 
- Constitutional Primitives
 
- Identity
 
- Attribute
 
- Relationship
 
- Structural Classification
 
- Ontological invariants
 
- Structural constraints
 
- Structural mapping of the constitutional Observer concept to the constitutional primitives
 

 
ZRM-002 MAY refine the constitutional meaning of the five primitives established by ZRM-001:
 
 
- Subject
 
- Object
 
- Place
 
- Event
 
- Evidence
 

 
ZRM-002 SHALL NOT redefine these primitives.
 
Structural Classification shall mean:
 
 
Assignment of a Primitive to an ontological category based solely upon immutable structural characteristics.
 
 
Structural Classification SHALL NOT introduce:
 
 
- Business meaning
 
- Behavioral meaning
 
- Semantic interpretation
 
- Execution logic
 
- Taxonomy beyond constitutional structure
