# ZRM-002B — Identity
 
## Constitutional Construct
 
### Draft v1.0
  
# 1. Constitutional Purpose
 
This Constitution defines the constitutional construct **Identity**.
 
It establishes the constitutional principles governing how every constitutional constituent of Reality is uniquely distinguished from every other constitutional constituent.
 
This Constitution answers one constitutional question only:
 
 
**"How is a constitutional constituent distinguished from every other constitutional constituent?"**
 
 
This Constitution SHALL NOT define identifiers, naming, storage, authentication, authorization, representations, semantic meaning, or implementation mechanisms.
  
# 2. Constitutional Authority
 
This Constitution derives its authority exclusively from:
 
 
- NORTH STAR
 
- Founding Principles
 
- ZRM Master Blueprint
 
- ZRM-001 — Reality Constitution
 
- ZRM-002A — Constitutional Primitives
 

 
If any statement conflicts with these constitutional authorities, those authorities SHALL prevail.
  
# 3. Constitutional Definition
 
**Identity** is a constitutional construct that uniquely distinguishes one constitutional constituent of Reality from every other constitutional constituent.
 
Identity is established at the moment a constitutional constituent comes into existence.
 
Identity is intrinsic to constitutional existence.
 
Identity SHALL NOT be created, assigned, interpreted, or modified by any observer, system, representation, or implementation.
 
Identity exists independently of:
 
 
- Representation
 
- Attributes
 
- Relationships
 
- Semantic interpretation
 
- Technology
 
- Observation
 

 
Identity distinguishes constitutional existence.

The Identity of an Event distinguishes the constitutional occurrence itself and SHALL remain independent of every Representation, Record, Log, Observation, or Evidence describing that occurrence.
 
Identity does not describe constitutional existence.
  
# 4. Constitutional Nature
 
Identity is a **Structural Constitutional Construct**.
 
Identity is **NOT** a Constitutional Primitive.
 
Identity exists only in relation to a constitutional constituent.
 
Every constitutional primitive possesses exactly one constitutional Identity.
 
Identity does not constitute Reality.
 
Identity distinguishes constituents that constitute Reality.
  
# 5. Constitutional Characteristics
 
Identity SHALL:
 
 
- uniquely distinguish one constitutional constituent;
 
- remain immutable throughout constitutional existence;
 
- exist independently of Representation;
 
- exist independently of Attributes;
 
- exist independently of Relationships;
 
- remain independent of semantic interpretation;
 
- remain independent of implementation technology;
 
- apply equally to Subject, Object, Place, Event, and Evidence.
 

 
Identity SHALL NOT:
 
 
- describe a constituent;
 
- classify a constituent;
 
- verify a constituent;
 
- authenticate a constituent;
 
- authorize a constituent;
 
- determine semantic meaning;
 
- change during constitutional existence.
 

  
# 6. Constitutional Invariants
 
## ID-1 — Identity Integrity
 
Identity SHALL remain Identity throughout constitutional existence.
 
No constitutional process SHALL transform Identity into any other constitutional construct.
  
## ID-2 — Uniqueness
 
Every constitutional constituent SHALL possess exactly one constitutional Identity.
 
No two constitutional constituents SHALL share the same constitutional Identity.
  
## ID-3 — Immutability
 
Identity is established upon constitutional existence.
 
Identity SHALL NEVER change.
 
Representations, Attributes, Relationships, and semantic classifications MAY change.
 
Identity SHALL NOT.
  
## ID-4 — Representation Independence
 
Identity exists independently of every Representation.
 
Representations MAY identify Identity.
 
Representations SHALL NEVER constitute Identity.
  
## ID-5 — Attribute Independence
 
Attributes describe constitutional constituents.
 
Identity distinguishes constitutional constituents.
 
No Attribute SHALL determine Identity.
 
No change to any Attribute SHALL modify Identity.
  
## ID-6 — Relationship Independence
 
Relationships connect constitutional constituents.
 
Relationships SHALL NOT create, modify, replace, merge, or divide Identity.
  
## ID-7 — Identity vs. Identifier
 
An **Identifier** is a Representation that refers to a constitutional Identity.
 
Identity is constitutional.
 
Identifiers are representational.
 
No Identifier SHALL be treated as Identity itself.
  
## ID-8 — Semantic Independence
 
Identity possesses no constitutional semantic meaning.
 
Identity SHALL NOT imply:
 
 
- Customer
 
- Employee
 
- Product
 
- Vehicle
 
- Sensor
 
- Invoice
 
- Asset
 
- Organization
 
- User
 

 
Semantic interpretation belongs exclusively to downstream constitutions.
  
# 7. Constitutional Relationships
 
Identity SHALL exist in relation to:
 
 
- Subject
 
- Object
 
- Place
 
- Event
 
- Evidence
 

 
Identity SHALL be referenced by Representations.
 
Identity SHALL be used by Relationships to distinguish participating constitutional constituents.
 
Identity SHALL NOT depend upon any Representation, Relationship, Attribute, or semantic projection for its constitutional existence.
  
# 8. Constitutional Non-Scope
 
This Constitution SHALL NOT define:
 
 
- UUIDs
 
- Database Primary Keys
 
- URIs
 
- DIDs
 
- QR Codes
 
- RFID Tags
 
- Cryptographic Hashes
 
- Digital Certificates
 
- Usernames
 
- Account Numbers
 
- Business Identifiers
 
- Authentication
 
- Authorization
 
- Storage
 
- Databases
 
- APIs
 
- Indexing
 
- Lookup Mechanisms
 
- Naming
 
- Labels
 
- Semantic Categories
 
- Business Meaning
 

 
These concerns belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.
  
# 9. Downstream Responsibilities
 
This Constitution delegates extension authority to:
 
 
- ZRM-002B — Relationships
 
- ZRM-002B — Attributes
 
- ZRM-003 — Reality Mathematics
 
- ZRM-005 — Semantic Projection
 
- ZRM-006 — Constitutional Boundaries
 
- ZRM-007 — Governance
 
- Capability Constitution
 
- Execution Constitution
 

 
No downstream Constitution may redefine Identity.
 
Downstream Constitutions MAY extend Identity only within the constitutional boundaries established herein.
  
# 10. Council Validation Questions
 
The AI Council SHALL verify:
 
 
1. Does Identity derive exclusively from ZRM-001 and the Constitutional Primitives?
 
2. Is Identity correctly classified as a Structural Constitutional Construct rather than a Primitive?
 
3. Does Identity remain unique and immutable?
 
4. Is Identity independent of Representation?
 
5. Is Identity independent of Attributes?
 
6. Is Identity independent of Relationships?
 
7. Is Identity independent of semantic interpretation?
 
8. Does the Constitution avoid implementation-specific language?
 
9. Does the Constitution preserve technology independence?
 
10. Does any downstream concern improperly appear within this Constitution?
 
11. Is the distinction between Identity and Identifier constitutionally preserved?
 
12. Does this Constitution answer only its single constitutional question?
 

 
All answers SHALL be affirmative before constitutional ratification.

# ZRM-002B — Relationships
 
## Constitutional Construct
 
### Draft v1.1 (Ratification Candidate)
  
# 1. Constitutional Purpose
 
This Constitution defines the constitutional construct **Relationship**.
 
It establishes the constitutional principles governing how constitutional constituents of Reality are structurally connected.
 
This Constitution answers one constitutional question only:
 
**"How are constitutional constituents connected?"**
 
This Constitution SHALL NOT define graph technologies, databases, edges, references, foreign keys, semantic meaning, business rules, workflows, execution logic, implementation mechanisms, storage systems, or execution behavior.
  
# 2. Constitutional Authority
 
This Constitution derives its authority exclusively from:
 
 
- NORTH STAR
 
- Founding Principles
 
- ZRM Master Blueprint
 
- ZRM-001 — Reality Constitution
 
- ZRM-002A — Constitutional Primitives
 
- Identity (this Constitution)
 

 
If any statement conflicts with these constitutional authorities, those authorities SHALL prevail.
  
# 3. Constitutional Definition
 
A **Relationship** is a constitutional construct that establishes a structural connection between two or more constitutional constituents of Reality.
 
Relationships do not create constitutional constituents.
 
Relationships connect constitutional constituents that already exist.
 
Relationships exist independently of:
 
 
- Representation
 
- Semantic interpretation
 
- Implementation technology
 
- Observation
 
- Storage mechanisms
 

 
Relationships connect constitutional existence.
 
Relationships do not define constitutional existence.
  
# 4. Constitutional Nature
 
Relationship is a **Structural Constitutional Construct**.
 
Its constitutional classification is **Structural Relationship**.
 
Relationship is **NOT** a Constitutional Primitive.
 
Relationships exist only between constitutional constituents possessing constitutional Identity.
 
Relationships neither constitute Reality nor modify Reality.
 
They express structural connectivity within Reality.
  
# 5. Constitutional Characteristics
 
Relationships SHALL:
 
 
- connect constitutional constituents;
 
- preserve the Identity of every participating constituent;
 
- exist independently of Representation;
 
- exist independently of semantic interpretation;
 
- exist independently of implementation technology;
 
- exist independently of Observation.
 

 
Relationships SHALL NOT:
 
 
- create constitutional constituents;
 
- destroy constitutional constituents;
 
- merge constitutional Identity;
 
- divide constitutional Identity;
 
- modify constitutional Identity;
 
- determine semantic meaning;
 
- perform execution.
 

  
# 6. Constitutional Invariants
 
## REL-1 — Relationship Integrity
 
A Relationship SHALL remain a Relationship throughout its constitutional existence.
 
No constitutional process SHALL transform a Relationship into any other constitutional construct.
  
## REL-2 — Identity Preservation
 
Relationships SHALL preserve the constitutional Identity of every participating constituent.
 
No Relationship SHALL create, merge, divide, replace, or modify constitutional Identity.
  
## REL-3 — Primitive Independence
 
Relationships SHALL NOT create constitutional Primitives.
 
Every participating constitutional constituent SHALL exist independently before participating in a Relationship.
  
## REL-4 — Representation Independence
 
Relationships exist independently of every Representation.
 
Representations MAY describe Relationships.
 
Representations SHALL NEVER constitute Relationships.
  
## REL-5 — Semantic Independence
 
Relationships possess no constitutional semantic meaning.
 
Business meaning SHALL belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.
  
## REL-6 — Technology Independence
 
Relationships SHALL remain independent of all execution mechanisms and storage technologies, including but not limited to:
 
 
- Graph databases
 
- Foreign keys
 
- References
 
- Object pointers
 
- APIs
 
- Storage engines
 
- Communication protocols
 

  
## REL-7 — Structural Neutrality
 
Relationships SHALL connect constitutional constituents without implying:
 
 
- Ownership
 
- Control
 
- Permission
 
- Dependency
 
- Hierarchy
 
- Causality
 
- Sequence
 

 
Such interpretations belong exclusively to downstream constitutions.

# ZRM-002B — Attributes

## Constitutional Construct

---

# 1. Constitutional Purpose

This Constitution defines the constitutional construct **Attribute**.

It establishes the constitutional principles governing how constitutional constructs of Reality are described.

This Constitution answers one constitutional question only:

> **"How is a constitutional construct described?"**

This Constitution SHALL NOT define semantic meaning, business concepts, classifications, naming, identifiers, validation rules, execution behavior, storage mechanisms, implementation technologies, database schemas, programming language properties, or representational formats.

---

# 2. Constitutional Authority

This Constitution derives its authority exclusively from:

- NORTH STAR
- Founding Principles
- ZRM Master Blueprint
- ZRM-001 — Reality Constitution
- ZRM-002A — Constitutional Primitives
- ZRM-002B — Identity
- ZRM-002B — Relationships

If any statement conflicts with these constitutional authorities, those authorities SHALL prevail.

---

# 3. Constitutional Definition

An **Attribute** is a constitutional construct that describes one aspect of exactly one constitutional construct.

Attributes describe constitutional existence.

They do not constitute constitutional existence.

Attributes exist only because the constitutional construct they describe exists.

Attributes SHALL describe exactly one constitutional construct.

That constitutional construct MAY be:

- a Constitutional Primitive; or
- a Relationship.

Attributes exist independently of:

- Representation
- Observation
- Semantic interpretation
- Implementation technology
- Storage mechanisms

Attributes do not distinguish constitutional constructs.

Attributes do not connect constitutional constructs.

Attributes describe constitutional constructs.

---

# 4. Constitutional Nature

Attribute is a **Structural Constitutional Construct**.

Attribute is **NOT** a Constitutional Primitive.

Attributes possess no independent constitutional existence.

Every Attribute SHALL belong to exactly one constitutional construct.

Attributes MAY describe:

- Subject
- Object
- Place
- Event
- Evidence
- Relationship

Attributes SHALL NOT exist independently of the constitutional construct they describe.

---

# 5. Constitutional Characteristics

Attributes SHALL:

- describe constitutional constructs;
- characterize constitutional constructs;
- express one descriptive aspect of a constitutional construct;
- belong to exactly one constitutional construct;
- exist independently of Representation;
- exist independently of semantic interpretation;
- exist independently of implementation technology;
- remain distinct from Identity;
- remain distinct from Relationships.

Attributes SHALL NOT:

- distinguish constitutional Identity;
- create constitutional constructs;
- destroy constitutional constructs;
- connect constitutional constructs;
- classify constitutional constructs;
- determine semantic meaning;
- perform execution;
- imply business meaning.

---

# 6. Constitutional Invariants

## ATR-1 — Attribute Integrity

An Attribute SHALL remain an Attribute throughout its constitutional existence.

No constitutional process SHALL transform an Attribute into any other constitutional construct.

---

## ATR-2 — Construct Dependence

Every Attribute SHALL belong to exactly one constitutional construct.

An Attribute SHALL NOT exist independently.

---

## ATR-3 — Identity Independence

Attributes describe constitutional constructs.

Identity distinguishes constitutional constructs.

No Attribute SHALL create, replace, merge, divide, or modify constitutional Identity.

Two constitutional constructs MAY possess identical Attributes while remaining constitutionally distinct through their respective Identities.

Likewise, a constitutional construct MAY change every Attribute it possesses while remaining constitutionally identical.

---

## ATR-4 — Relationship Independence

Attributes describe.

Relationships connect.

No Attribute SHALL create, replace, modify, or eliminate a Relationship.

Likewise, no Relationship SHALL replace the descriptive function of an Attribute.

When an Attribute belongs to a Relationship, that Attribute SHALL describe the Relationship itself.

It SHALL NOT describe the participating constitutional constructs.

---

## ATR-5 — Representation Independence

Attributes exist independently of every Representation.

Representations MAY describe Attributes.

Representations SHALL NEVER constitute Attributes.

---

## ATR-6 — Mutability

Attributes MAY change during the constitutional existence of the constitutional construct they describe.

Such changes SHALL NOT affect constitutional Identity.

---

## ATR-7 — Semantic Independence

Attributes possess no constitutional semantic meaning.

An Attribute SHALL NOT imply:

- Customer
- Employee
- Product
- Vehicle
- Asset
- Invoice
- Organization
- User
- VIP
- Active
- Premium

Business interpretation belongs exclusively to downstream constitutions.

---

## ATR-8 — Technology Independence

Attributes SHALL remain independent of all implementation technologies, including but not limited to:

- Database columns
- Object properties
- JSON fields
- XML elements
- Programming language members
- Storage schemas
- Serialization formats
- APIs
- Messaging protocols
