## Why Now
 
Several independent technological and market shifts are converging for the first time, creating the conditions for a new category of infrastructure.
 
Physical objects are becoming digitally addressable through QR codes, NFC, GS1 Digital Links, digital product passports, and connected devices.
 
Artificial intelligence is transforming software from tools that display information into agents that make decisions and execute actions. These agents require trusted, machine-readable identities and contextual understanding of the entities they interact with.
 
Governments and industries are increasingly demanding transparency, traceability, authentication, and verifiable product information across supply chains.
 
At the same time, organizations continue to rely on fragmented systems that record isolated events but fail to preserve the intent and context behind interactions.
 
The result is a growing gap between what interactions mean and what systems understand.
 
Zyppi exists to close that gap.
 
Just as the internet required protocols for information exchange, the emerging physical-digital economy requires infrastructure for intent exchange.
 
The opportunity is not the growth of QR codes. The opportunity is the emergence of a world where every interaction, object, and identity can become understandable, trustworthy, and actionable.

## The Five Fundamental Concepts

Every Zyppi interaction can be understood through five fundamental concepts.

### Touchpoint
The physical or digital entry point through which interaction begins.

### Identity
The entity being resolved, referenced, or interacted with.

### Intent
The desired outcome that motivates the interaction.

### Transaction
The sequence of actions required to fulfill the intent.

### Outcome
The result produced by the completed transaction.

- Most systems stop at touchpoints.

- Some systems understand identities.

- Few systems understand transactions.

> Zyppi is designed to understand and preserve the complete chain from touchpoint to outcome.
