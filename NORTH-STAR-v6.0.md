# Universal Reality Model

Every interaction Zyppi perceives exists within **five constitutional dimensions.** This section supersedes the three-dimension model (Human / Object / Place) used in North Star v5.0. See `00-RECONCILIATION-NOTE.md` for the full rationale.

## Subject

An autonomous constituent of reality — anything capable of initiating, holding standing, or acting with intent.

Includes:

- Human
- Organization (acting through delegated human authority)
- AI Agent
- Autonomous System
- Future autonomous entities not yet invented

Every Human is a Subject. Not every Subject is a Human. This broadening is intentional: Zyppi's "AI Native" principle (below) already assumes AI Agents carry standing and authority comparable to humans within constitutional bounds — Subject is the name for the category both belong to.

Concerned with:

- Identity
- Authority
- Standing
- Delegation
- Credentials
- Trust

## Object

A non-autonomous constituent of reality that participates through relationship with a Subject, not through its own intent.

Includes:

- Products
- Assets
- Devices
- Documents
- Vehicles
- Packages
- Digital Twins

Concerned with:

- Authenticity
- Ownership
- Lifecycle

## Place

The spatial or contextual environment within which Subjects and Objects exist and interact.

Includes:

- Stores
- Hospitals
- Airports
- Factories
- Borders
- Warehouses
- Homes

Concerned with:

- Jurisdiction
- Context
- Presence

## Event

A change occurring within reality, involving one or more Subjects, Objects, or Places.

Events are how reality becomes knowable at all — nothing is perceived except through something happening. An Event is not itself proof; it is the occurrence that Evidence later attests to.

## Evidence

The trace an Event leaves behind — the material from which Trust is computed.

Evidence may originate from:

- QR
- NFC
- Government credentials
- Verifiable Credentials
- BLE
- GPS
- Biometrics
- Digital Signatures
- IoT
- Sensors
- Enterprise Systems
- Future trust technologies

Zyppi is carrier-agnostic. Any trusted Evidence becomes a constitutional input.

Reality emerges from the relationships between all five dimensions across time.
